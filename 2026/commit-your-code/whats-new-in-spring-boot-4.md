# What's new in Spring Boot 4

## Abstract

The next major release of Spring is here, and this session is your fast guide to what matters most. Spring Boot 4 delivers performance gains, streamlined developer workflows, and production-ready features that solve real-world challenges.
We'll walk through the highlights: HTTP interfaces, JSpecify null safety, Jackson 3 integration, and the built-in resilience patterns that make your applications production-ready from day one. You'll leave knowing what's new, what's worth your attention, and where to dig deeper after the session.
