# Fundamentals of Sofware Engineering in the age of AI

## Abstract 

Agentic coding assistants and AI-powered editors are reshaping how we build software, leading some to proclaim the end of software engineering. Is it time to explore other careers? Not so fast. The rumors of our demise are greatly exaggerated. These tools can boost productivity, but using them effectively still requires mastering the fundamentals of the craft.

Based on our book Fundamentals of Software Engineering, this 90-minute session bridges the gap between what early-career developers learn in formal education and what they need to thrive in professional environments where human expertise and AI increasingly collaborate. We'll focus on the durable skills that remain relevant regardless of changing languages, frameworks, and AI capabilities.

Through a mix of conceptual teaching, real-world examples, and group discussion, attendees will leave with a clearer framework for becoming well-rounded software engineers, along with the discernment to know when and how to integrate AI tools into their workflow.
