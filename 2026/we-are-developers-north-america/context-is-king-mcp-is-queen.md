# If Context is King, MCP is Queen

## Abstract

You ask an LLM a question about your data. It guesses. It hallucinates. It gets it wrong. Sound familiar?

Context is king. Without it, even the best models fall apart. The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is how you give your AI the context it needs by connecting it to your applications, your databases, and your tools.

In this session, we'll start with the problem: a client asks a question and fails. Then we'll connect an MCP server and watch the same question get answered correctly. That's the power of context.

From there, we'll walk through how MCP works under the hood. You'll see how servers and clients communicate, what the protocol actually does, and what it takes to build your own. Examples will use Spring AI, but the concepts apply regardless of your language or framework.

You'll leave understanding what MCP is, why it matters, and how to start connecting your applications to the world of AI.
